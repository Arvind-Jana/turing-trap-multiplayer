### c. Project Description

**Short Pitch (1-2 sentences):**
*Turing Trap is a real-time multiplayer social deduction party game where friends compete to sniff out the undercover AI agent among human responses to quirky career and college scenarios.*

**Detailed Overview:**
Inspired by the social comedy of *Jackbox Games* and the tension of *Among Us*, **Turing Trap** tackles one of the defining questions of our generation: *Can you tell human creativity apart from artificial intelligence?*

Players join a room via a 4-letter code on mobile or desktop. Each round delivers a creative prompt centered on modern student life, tech interviews, or workplace dilemmas (e.g., *"Why should this company hire you instead of a hyper-intelligent calculator?"* or *"What is your 3:00 AM excuse to an email professor for an extension?"*). 

While human players type their witty responses, an undercover AI generates an answer in secret. All answers are then shuffled and presented anonymously. Players have 30 seconds to debate, analyze the nuances, and cast their votes on **who is the bot**. 

**Core Features & Highlights:**
1. **Real-Time Multiplayer Architecture**: Built with Node.js and Socket.io with an authoritative room state machine, synchronized countdown timers, and seamless reconnections.
2. **AI Literacy as Gameplay**: Prompts encourage players to examine hallmarks of LLM generation (hallucinations, excessive politeness, structural rhythm) versus genuine human wit.
3. **Inclusive Solo & Group Play**: Includes an intelligent "Simulated Player" engine so individual reviewers or judges can test the full multiplayer game loop end-to-end with simulated friends at the click of a button.
4. **Stunning Audio-Visual Polish**: Built-in Web Audio API sound synthesis (buzzer, fanfare, tension ticks), canvas confetti particle engine, responsive glassmorphism dark theme, and an animated podium celebration.
